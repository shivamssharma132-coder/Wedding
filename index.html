The Setting

Where heritage meets celebration

Itachuna Rajbari is a centuries-old zamindar palace in rural West Bengal, with courtyards, terracotta arches and a Durga Dalan that has witnessed generations of celebration. Its weathered stone and open skies make it less a venue, more a backdrop written for this exact story.

Guests are welcomed into a world of carved colonnades, candlelit courtyards and quiet gardens — a setting that needs very little decoration to feel cinematic.

Heritage Courtyards

Three interconnected courtyards, each suited to a different celebration.

On-Site Stay

Period rooms and suites available for close family and outstation guests.

Easy Access

2.5 hours from Kolkata, with shuttle service arranged on event days.
